Snake Support

iPhone · iOS 16 or later · Games / Arcade

This page is support for Snake, a retro arcade game. Steer with the D-pad or a swipe, eat fruit, grow, and chase a high score. There is no account. Scores stay on your iPhone.

Getting started

  • Tap Play. The snake moves on its own — turn with the D-pad or a swipe.
  • Eat fruit to grow and score 10 points. The snake speeds up after every bite.
  • Hit a wall or your own tail and the run is over. Fill the board to win.
  • Settings has sound, haptics, grid, difficulty, snake color, and fruit style.
  • Rate Snake from Settings or About. After a few games, the app may also show Apple’s rating card on its own.

High scores

The app keeps the top 10 scores on this iPhone. Deleting the app also deletes those scores.

Ads

A Google AdMob banner can appear on the menu, high scores, settings, and About screens. A full-screen ad may show after Game Over so the app can stay free. iOS may ask for tracking permission so ads can be more relevant. Allow or decline; the app still works either way.

Authorized ad sellers for Sai Laksha Technologies apps are listed at https://sreedharlakshman2.github.io/app-ads.txt. That root file is what Google AdMob uses to verify this app.

Privacy

Scores and settings stay on this device. See the Privacy Policy.
